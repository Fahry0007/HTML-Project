An event is something special that people celebrate or enjoy together. It can be small, like a birthday party, or big, like a wedding. Birthdays are fun events with cakes, games, and gifts. Weddings are grand events where families and friends gather to celebrate. Schools have events like sports day and annual day, where students participate in activities. Festivals like Christmas, Diwali, and Eid are big events filled with joy, decorations, and delicious food. A book fair is an event where people find and buy books. Picnics are fun outdoor events with food and games. People prepare for events by planning, decorating, and inviting guests. Events bring happiness and create beautiful memories. They help people feel closer to each other. Every event is special in its way. Events make life more exciting and joyful.
